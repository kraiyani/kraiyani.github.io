About

Research mindset.
Engineering practice.

I’m a data scientist working at the intersection of applied machine learning, research and software engineering. My work spans financial decisioning, language technology and earth observation, with a common thread: turning a complex question into a system people can use.

At EXUS, I develop machine learning and applied AI capabilities for the EXUS Financial Suite, alongside technical delivery and collaboration with clients, product teams and engineers.

Before that, I worked on European research projects at UNINOVA and the University of Évora. My PhD explored uncertainty prediction for active learning in earth observation: how to make better use of limited labelled data.
